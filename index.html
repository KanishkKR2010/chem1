How VSEPR works

Count electron domains around the central atom. Each bond—single, double or triple—counts as one domain. Lone pairs also count as one domain and repel more strongly than bonding pairs.

Angle rule

The displayed angle is the ideal VSEPR value or the commonly observed approximate angle for common lone-pair geometries. Lone pairs generally compress adjacent bond angles.

What holds molecules together?

Quick decision checklist

  1. All molecules experience London dispersion forces.
  2. Polar molecules also experience dipole–dipole interactions.
  3. Hydrogen bonding requires H directly attached to N, O or F and an available acceptor.

Formal charge

FC = valence electrons − nonbonding electrons − ½(bonding electrons)

Octet rule

Many main-group atoms tend toward eight electrons around the central atom. Common exceptions include H (duet), Be and B electron-deficient compounds, and expanded valence shells for some period-3 and heavier atoms.

HYBRIDIZATION

Orbitals & Hybridization

Core idea

Hybridization is a model that describes mixing atomic orbitals to generate equivalent directional orbitals useful for explaining molecular geometry and bonding.

2 domains → sp → linear → 180°
3 domains → sp² → trigonal planar → 120°
4 domains → sp³ → tetrahedral → 109.5°
5 domains → sp³d → trigonal bipyramidal
6 domains → sp³d² → octahedral

How to read this map

Across a period, effective nuclear charge generally increases. Atomic radius tends to decrease, while first ionization energy and electronegativity generally increase. Down a group, added electron shells usually increase atomic size and shield outer electrons.
